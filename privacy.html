PhotoLoop

PhotoLoop is designed to help you review and organize photos on your iPhone. This policy describes the intended privacy practices for the first version of the app.

Photos stay on your device

PhotoLoop is designed to analyze your photo library on your iPhone using Apple frameworks. Your photos and videos are not uploaded to LoopTool AI servers, because the app does not use a photo-upload service.

Photo access

PhotoLoop needs access to your Photos library to identify duplicates, similar photos, screenshots, and potentially blurry images. You choose whether to grant access in iOS Settings. You can change or revoke that permission at any time.

No account required

The planned MVP does not require an account, sign-in, cloud sync, advertising, or a subscription. We do not intend to collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or photo library contents inside the app.

Deleting photos

PhotoLoop will only request deletion after you review and confirm your selection. Deletions are handled by Apple Photos and remain subject to its Recently Deleted recovery period.

Website data

This website is a static site. It does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, login forms, or embedded social widgets. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in that message.

Changes to this policy

When the app moves from development to release, we will update this page to describe the final features and any data practices that differ from this plan. We will publish the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to privacy@looptoolai.com.
